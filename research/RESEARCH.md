# IFM stealth game: reference research

Compiled 2026-11-05 for a 2D top-down pixel-art stealth game (Escapists-style) set at the Institut Français de la Mode, Paris. The aim is accurate layout, not background. Every claim has its source beside it. Anything I worked out myself (from plans, OSM geometry or photos) is marked **[inference]**.

No requests were made to Instagram, Facebook, Threads or any other Meta domain. No Instagram leads came up, so none are listed.

Images are in `images/`. There is an index at the end.

---

## 1. Location and exterior

### Address and building

- **IFM postal address: 36 quai d'Austerlitz, 75013 Paris.** Sources: [Campus France IFM sheet](https://ressources.campusfrance.org/pratique/etablissements/en/art_ifm_en.pdf), [Routard](https://www.routard.com/fr/guide/france/ile-de-france/paris/13eme-arrondissement/cite-de-la-mode-et-du-design) ("l'IFM est installé au 36 quai d'Austerlitz depuis 2008").
- **The building, Les Docks – Cité de la Mode et du Design, is at 34 quai d'Austerlitz.** Sources: [citemodedesign.fr](https://www.citemodedesign.fr/en/the-cite), [Wikipedia (IFM)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institut_Fran%C3%A7ais_de_la_Mode). The address numbers run **32 / 34 / 36 along one building**. The Cité's site gives a step-free **lift at 36 quai d'Austerlitz ("IFM side")** and an **access ramp at 32**. Source: [citemodedesign.fr](https://www.citemodedesign.fr/en/the-cite).
- **Your premise is confirmed.** The building is the green "Plug-Over" by **Jakob + MacFarlane**, a conversion of the 1907 Magasins Généraux concrete warehouse (architect Georges Morin-Goustiaux). It was won in a 2004 competition and work started in 2007. Sources: [Jakob+MacFarlane](https://jakobmacfarlane.com/en/projects/architecture/the-docks-city-of-fashion-design), [ArchDaily](https://www.archdaily.com/21151/docks-de-paris-jakob-macfarlane), [irenebrination](https://www.irenebrination.com/2011/11/10/jakobmacfarlane/). Completion is given as 2008 to 2012 depending on the source. The public opening was in 2010. Sources: [archiweb](https://www.archiweb.cz/en/b/parizske-centrum-designu-a-mody), [Wikipedia (Cité)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cit%C3%A9_de_la_mode_et_du_design).
- **Coordinates:** about 48.8408 N, 2.3700 E. In OpenStreetMap the building is way 78535563, "Les Docks – Cité de la Mode et du Design", 34 Quai d'Austerlitz. [OSM way 78535563](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/78535563).

### Has IFM moved or expanded since 2019?

- **IFM has not moved to another site. It expanded inside the same building.** It merged with the École de la Chambre Syndicale de la Couture Parisienne (ECSCP), which was previously at **rue Réaumur** in 2,350 m². IFM used to have 2,300 m² in the Docks. Together they now have "près de 8000 m²" there. Works began in spring 2019 and the move was planned for the 2020 rentrée. Source: [IFM press kit, FHCM PDF](https://fhcm.paris/media/news/files/dossier-de-presse-institut-francais-de-la-mode.pdf).
- IFM moved into the new campus in **January 2021**. Source: [ifmparis.fr](https://www.ifmparis.fr/en/news/le-nouveau-campus-de-linstitut-francais-de-la-mode). It was inaugurated on **24 Nov 2021** by Bruno Le Maire. The campus is 8,000 m², with "an extra 1,000 square meters" planned for autumn 2022. Source: [FashionUnited](https://fashionunited.com/education/news/institut-francais-de-la-mode-ifm-opens-new-campus/2021112944084). A 2026 article gives **9,000 m²** inside the Cité's 15,000 m², which suggests the extension was built. Source: [NSS Magazine](https://www.nssmag.com/en/fashion/47508/institut-francais-de-la-mode-40-years).
- **The 2021 interior fit-out was by Architecture Patrick Mauger, not Jakob+MacFarlane.** FashionUnited credits J+M, but that refers to the building. Mauger's project data: client SCI Docks en Seine, 8,050 m², €14.6M, "Completion: 2021 (1st part)". Sources: [AECCafe ArchShowcase, archived](http://web.archive.org/web/20210713134220/https://www10.aeccafe.com/blogs/arch-showcase/2021/07/11/international-fashion-campus-in-paris-france-by-architecture-patrick-mauger/), [Archinect](https://archinect.com/architecturepatrickmauger/project/international-fashion-campus), [WorldArchitecture](https://worldarchitecture.org/architecture-projects/ppznn/french-institute-of-fashion-paris-project-pages.html), [Archilovers](https://archilovers.com/projects/289326/international-fashion-campus-paris.html).

### Which part of the building IFM occupies

- Architectural Record says the IFM "is at the building's western end". Source: [Architectural Record](https://www.architecturalrecord.com/articles/12446-the-utilitarian-docks-de-paris-makes-a-fashion-statement). Two other sources agree: the "IFM side" lift is at no. 36 ([citemodedesign.fr](https://www.citemodedesign.fr/en/the-cite)), and 2025 street photos show the "INSTITUT FRANÇAIS DE LA MODE" lettering on the NW part of the quai façade (see the entrance section).
- **[inference]** The building runs NW to SE along the quai. Its NW end faces Gare d'Austerlitz and Pont Charles-de-Gaulle, so "western end" means the **NW end, the station end**. At 8,000 to 9,000 m² of a 15,000 to 20,000 m² building, IFM probably takes roughly the western half on several levels. The east/SE end (no. 32) holds the nightlife venues.

### Geometry and structure (for blocking out the level)

- Original warehouse: **3 storeys, four pavilions**, each with **one 10 m bay and four 7.5 m bays**, so 4 × 40 m = 160 m. At quai level the 10 m bay is **reachable from the street**. The other bays are about **1.25 m higher** (the French text says about 1.5 m). Sources: [archilovers](https://archilovers.com/projects/52867/les-docks.html), [e-architect](https://www.e-architect.com/paris/docks-de-paris).
- Length about **525 ft (160 m)**, area 170,000 sq ft. The bay rhythm is "four 25-ft bays separated by a single 33-ft bay". Source: [Architectural Record](https://www.architecturalrecord.com/articles/12446-the-utilitarian-docks-de-paris-makes-a-fashion-statement).
- **[inference from OSM polygon]** The footprint, including the Plug-Over, is about **170 m × 39 m**. The long axis has a bearing of about 138° (NW to SE). The Seine is on the NE side and quai d'Austerlitz on the SW side. A good scale would be **1 tile = 1 m**, which gives a building about 170 × 39 tiles.
- **Two ground levels.** The cross sections show a **street level** (quai d'Austerlitz) and a lower **embankment / bas-port level** beside the Seine. Above them are the upper floors and the **roof deck**. See `images/docks_cross_sections_JMF_hires.jpg` and the source, [area-arch](https://www.area-arch.it/en/?p=45017).

### Façade materials and colours

- **Plug-Over:** "a warped and faceted protuberance of **green-fritted glass**" that wraps the roof and the river façade. Steel tube portal frames (6.6 in Ø) are hung at 8-ft intervals. There are 630 planar glass panels. The existing frame is **raw sandblasted concrete**. The roof is **oak decking** with landscaping. Source: [Architectural Record](https://www.architecturalrecord.com/articles/12446-the-utilitarian-docks-de-paris-makes-a-fashion-statement). The listed materials are glass, steel, wood decking and planting, and the building is bright green by day. Source: [Jakob+MacFarlane](https://jakobmacfarlane.com/en/projects/architecture/the-docks-city-of-fashion-design).
- Night lighting is by **Yann Kersalé**. Sources: [Jakob+MacFarlane](https://jakobmacfarlane.com/en/projects/architecture/the-docks-city-of-fashion-design), [NSS](https://www.nssmag.com/en/fashion/47508/institut-francais-de-la-mode-40-years).
- **Quai (street) side, observed in street-level photos from 2022, 2025 and Oct 2026:** grey concrete pillars and spandrels with large dark-framed glazing on the ground floor. Above that, the street façade has **opaque lime/olive-green metal cladding panels** (not glass), which sweep down in a diagonal ramp shape. Plane trees line the pavement, with low black X-pattern barriers between the trees. Sources: `images/pano360_quai_*`, [Panoramax](https://api.panoramax.xyz/api/collections/5e39e224-3b59-4df0-a059-0b96768931b6/items/9b9240c7-8af3-4fcb-b111-90ddf8f9e731). OSM tags the wall colour as green: [OSM way 78535563](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/78535563).
- **Street layout in front, observed:** quai d'Austerlitz has 2 general lanes, a **bus lane** and a **two-way cycle track**, with a 30 km/h limit. Sources: [OSM way 647730738](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/647730738), [OSM way 728029441](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/728029441). **Bus stops "Cité de la Mode et du Design"** (lines **61** and **215**) are right in front of the building. Sources: [OSM node 6360833599](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/6360833599), [OSM node 2949611366](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/2949611366).
- **Across the quai, SW side:** a curved glass office block of 7 levels, tagged "Caisse des dépôts" in OSM ([OSM way 62380201](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/62380201)). It appears as the tall building in the J+M cross section and in every street photo.

### Riverside promenade

- A continuous public route runs from the **lowest level by the Seine, up through the building to the roof deck and back down**. Sources: [e-architect](https://www.e-architect.com/paris/docks-de-paris), [archiweb](https://www.archiweb.cz/en/b/parizske-centrum-designu-a-mody).
- **Observed, 2022 panoramas:** the embankment level is a **cobbled quay**. Under the building there is a **concrete arcade of arched beams**, with metal **roller shutters** (club and venue fronts), graffiti on the pillars, low concrete benches and mooring bollards, and the Seine immediately to the NE. A ramp/stair climbs to street level at the NW end. Sources: `images/pano360_embankment_*`, [Panoramax search, riverside bbox](https://api.panoramax.xyz/api/search?bbox=2.3692,48.8410,2.3712,48.8420&limit=60) (the item ID prefixes are in the image index).
- The **Port d'Austerlitz** marina and houseboats are moored alongside. One example is the restaurant barge "Le Jardin Sauvage", which is also numbered 36 quai d'Austerlitz. Sources: [OSM way 294641170](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/294641170), [OSM Port d'Austerlitz](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/200164053). New river-bus and water-taxi stops connect to street level. Source: [Architectural Record](https://www.architecturalrecord.com/articles/12446-the-utilitarian-docks-de-paris-makes-a-fashion-statement).
- There is a public through-passage **under a green overhang at "34 QUAI D'AUSTERLITZ"**: a wide **concrete stair goes down from the pavement towards the river level, behind a sliding metal gate**. It is about mid-building, SE of the IFM lettering. Source: `images/pano360_quai_no34_public_passage_gate_steps_down_to_seine_2022_CCBYSA.jpg`, [Panoramax f4db12dc](https://api.panoramax.xyz/api/collections/21a1500e-3200-4321-96ec-9a7ad163c190/items/f4db12dc-199f-44dd-84ea-db02ad028298). **[inference]** This would make a good "public" route in the game that skips the IFM gates and leads to the riverside.

### Roof terrace

- The roof is a planted, faceted landscape with **oak/timber decks**. The Plug-Over tube adds a new top floor and covered walkways. Sources: [Jakob+MacFarlane](https://jakobmacfarlane.com/en/projects/architecture/the-docks-city-of-fashion-design), [ArchDaily](https://www.archdaily.com/21151/docks-de-paris-jakob-macfarlane). There is a rooftop restaurant. Source: [Architectural Record](https://www.architecturalrecord.com/articles/12446-the-utilitarian-docks-de-paris-makes-a-fashion-statement). Photos: `images/commons_roof_*`, `images/docks_roof_terrace_deck_view_west_to_bridges_Borel.jpg`.

## 2. Walking routes

The distances come from the OSM foot router (routing.openstreetmap.de) and street names from Nominatim reverse geocoding. Each route ends at the covered footway into the NW end of the Docks (48.84121, 2.36903). Walking times assume about 4.5 to 5 km/h.

### A. Gare d'Austerlitz, quai-side (south) exit → IFM: about 280 m, 4 min

| # | Segment | Length |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Leave the station's river-side frontage onto **Quai d'Austerlitz** (85 quai d'Austerlitz) | ~30 m |
| 2 | Walk SE along the Quai d'Austerlitz pavement, with the station on your right and the Seine/Port d'Austerlitz on your left | ~150 m |
| 3 | Pass under/beside the south abutment of **Pont Charles-de-Gaulle**, then continue on Quai d'Austerlitz | ~100 m |
| 4 | Reach the NW corner of the Docks and the covered passage / "entrée 34" | ~10 m |

Source: router output. Station location: [OSM / Nominatim, "Gare d'Austerlitz, 85 Quai d'Austerlitz"](https://nominatim.openstreetmap.org/ui/search.html?q=Gare+d%27Austerlitz).

### B. Gare d'Austerlitz, main hall on Boulevard de l'Hôpital → IFM: about 610 m, 8 min

| # | Segment | Length |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Leave the station on **Boulevard de l'Hôpital** | ~35 m |
| 2 | Walk north on Bd de l'Hôpital to the Seine (Place Valhubert side) | ~195 m |
| 3 | Turn right onto **Quai d'Austerlitz** and pass the station's river frontage | ~270 m |
| 4 | Continue SE past Pont Charles-de-Gaulle to the Docks NW entrance | ~100 m |

### C. Gare de Lyon → IFM via Pont Charles-de-Gaulle: about 780 m, 10 to 11 min (the direct route)

| # | Segment | Length |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Leave the station (Hall 1 / clock-tower side) onto **Rue de Chalon** and **Place Louis-Armand** | ~130 m |
| 2 | Take **Rue de Bercy** briefly SW | ~130 m |
| 3 | Walk down **Rue Van Gogh** to the river | ~155 m |
| 4 | Cross **Quai de la Rapée** (right bank, Port de la Rapée moorings, Café Barge houseboat) | ~40 m |
| 5 | Cross **Pont Charles-de-Gaulle**, a flat girder bridge, to the left bank | ~210 m |
| 6 | Turn left onto **Quai d'Austerlitz** and walk SE to the Docks NW entrance | ~100 m |

Sources: router output. Bridge: [OSM way 183600873](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/183600873). Café Barge: [OSM way 141240918](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/141240918).

### D. Gare de Lyon → IFM via Pont d'Austerlitz: about 1.4 km, 18 to 19 min (the long alternative)

This goes NW along the right bank, crosses **Pont d'Austerlitz** to Place Valhubert, then follows route B. Source: router output. Only use it if Pont Charles-de-Gaulle is closed in the story.

## 3. Entrance and security

### Street entrance

- On the **quai d'Austerlitz façade**, the ground-floor glazing between the concrete pillars has large white lettering: **"entrée 34"** and **"Institut FRANÇAIS de la MODE"**. It is at the **NW part of the building**: the camera was at 48.84101, 2.36919 in May 2025. In 2022 the lettering was "entrée 34" with an arrow and no IFM name. Sources: [Panoramax 9b9240c7, 2025](https://api.panoramax.xyz/api/collections/5e39e224-3b59-4df0-a059-0b96768931b6/items/9b9240c7-8af3-4fcb-b111-90ddf8f9e731), [Panoramax b870bd03, 2022](https://api.panoramax.xyz/api/collections/21a1500e-3200-4321-96ec-9a7ad163c190/items/b870bd03-e366-4915-9c09-677351dd4eda). Images: `pano360_quai_facade_IFM_lettering_entree34_2025_etalab.jpg`, `pano360_quai_NW_corner_entree34_arrow_2022_CCBYSA.jpg`.
- Mauger's design: "a broad stepped approach from the quai into a bright, communal central space", which it calls "the new institutional threshold". Source: [WorldArchitecture](https://worldarchitecture.org/architecture-projects/ppznn/french-institute-of-fashion-paris-project-pages.html). "On the ground floor, the interior street created enables the school to open on the city." Source: [AECCafe, archived](http://web.archive.org/web/20210713134220/https://www10.aeccafe.com/blogs/arch-showcase/2021/07/11/international-fashion-campus-in-paris-france-by-architecture-patrick-mauger/).
- The step-free lift is at **36 quai d'Austerlitz ("IFM side")**. Source: [citemodedesign.fr](https://www.citemodedesign.fr/en/the-cite).

### Gates, badge reader and desk (from the architect's photo)

The 2021 photograph by Michel Denancé, published by Mauger on [Archinect](https://archinect.com/architecturepatrickmauger/project/international-fashion-campus), shows the IFM entrance lobby. It is saved as `ifm_entrance_lobby_speed_gates_and_reception_desk_Mauger_Denance.jpg`, with a close crop in `ifm_entrance_speed_gates_glass_wings_badge_reader_CROP_Mauger_Denance.jpg`. **[observed]**

- A bank of **stainless-steel pedestal speed gates with clear glass swing wings**. Count them from the photo: it looks like **about 3 lanes**, and one lane is wider, probably the accessible lane.
- At least one post has a **badge-reader head with a blue screen** (the left post). The others have **round white status lamps** on top.
- Beside the gates is a **white monolithic reception / security desk**. It is tucked under a **white sculptural staircase**, with a small fold-out writing shelf.
- Behind the gates is a corridor of **programme posters**: "Bachelor of Arts in Fashion Design", "MA in Fashion Design", "Majors in Leather Goods & Footwear", and the tagline **"Création. Management. Savoir-faire."**
- Finishes: raw concrete beams and columns, exposed cable trays and conduits, track spotlights and **light oak parquet**. Source for the materials: [WorldArchitecture](https://worldarchitecture.org/architecture-projects/ppznn/french-institute-of-fashion-paris-project-pages.html).
- A **ceiling-mounted dome CCTV camera** is visible in the ground-floor hall photo (`ifm_interior_ground_floor_interior_street_cafe_tables_cctv_Mauger_Denance.jpg`).

**Not found online:** the exact number of lanes, the door-to-gate distance, guard staffing, opening hours, and visitor badge procedure. No student blog, article or official page describes how the gates work. My searches found only generic access-control vendor pages, none about IFM. It is **not confirmed that the lobby in the photo is directly behind "entrée 34"** **[inference: likely]**.

## 4. Floor plans

### Found: whole-building drawings by Jakob + MacFarlane (2009 to 2012 state)

These are from [area-arch](https://www.area-arch.it/en/?p=45017), at 1280 px. Lower-resolution copies are on [archiweb](https://www.archiweb.cz/en/b/parizske-centrum-designu-a-mody).

- `docks_PLANS_siteplan_firstfloor_embankment_JMF_hires.jpg`: **site plan, first floor plan and embankment plan**, with a scale bar (0 / 5 / 10 m). In the drawing the Seine is at the top, quai d'Austerlitz is at the bottom and the NW end is on the left **[inference from orientation]**. The first floor plan has cores, stairs and lifts at both ends (the left/NW end is denser, which is where IFM is) and a large open column-grid hall in the middle. The embankment plan shows a stepped **auditorium / tiered-seat room at the NW end**, a long ramp down to the river at the far left, and two big square stair voids.
- `docks_cross_sections_JMF_hires.jpg`: two **cross sections**. They show the street on one side, the lower embankment on the Seine side, the Plug-Over tube with stairs on the river face, and the tall office block across the quai.
- `docks_elevations_seine_quai_longitudinal_section_JMF_hires.jpg`: **elevations on the Seine and on quai d'Austerlitz, plus a longitudinal section**. The quai elevation shows the zig-zag **diagonal stair ramps** of the Plug-Over across the street façade.
- `docks_plugover_structure_studies_JMF.jpg`: studies of the steel tube geometry.

### Found: interior photos of the 2021 IFM campus (Patrick Mauger, © Michel Denancé)

These are from [Archinect](https://archinect.com/architecturepatrickmauger/project/international-fashion-campus).

- Central hall with a **large timber grand staircase**, the "organising spine" ([WorldArchitecture](https://worldarchitecture.org/architecture-projects/ppznn/french-institute-of-fashion-paris-project-pages.html)), double-height with galleries.
- Ground-floor "interior street" with café tables and benches.
- **Amphitheatre** with tiered fixed seating and an oak-clad back wall.
- Glazed classrooms off corridors, an open study floor with **bleacher steps**, a **sewing atelier** with industrial machines, and a **couture studio with mannequins and full-height windows over the Seine**.
- The facilities list includes a Fab lab, Knit Workshop, Leather goods & Footwear Studio, Photography Studio, Craft Station and Library. Source: [ifmparis.fr](https://www.ifmparis.fr/en/news/le-nouveau-campus-de-linstitut-francais-de-la-mode). An auditorium, CAD lab, classrooms and design studios are also mentioned. Source: [FashionUnited](https://fashionunited.com/education/news/institut-francais-de-la-mode-ifm-opens-new-campus/2021112944084).
- Mauger's layout: shared and public functions (**library, auditorium, amphitheatre, through-hall**) are at ground level, and workshops and teaching rooms are on the upper levels. "Three levels in total." Source: [WorldArchitecture](https://worldarchitecture.org/architecture-projects/ppznn/french-institute-of-fashion-paris-project-pages.html).

The speakers were Xavier Romatet (managing director), Sidney Toledano (chair) and Paris mayor Emmanuel Grégoire. Enrolment has grown from 749 students in 2019 to 1,400 in 2026. Source: [FashionUnited FR, 5 Nov 2026](https://fashionunited.fr/actualite/mode/linstitut-francais-de-la-mode-fete-ses-quarante-ans-la-soiree-en-images/2026110543477). ## 6. Reference games and asset packs

### Open-source games and code to read

I found no mature, popular open-source JavaScript Escapists clone. Most JS stealth repos are small, recent and sometimes AI-assisted, so **read them for patterns, not as engines to fork**.

| Repo | Stack / licence | Worth studying for |
|---|---|---|
| [AbdelStark/heist-one](https://github.com/AbdelStark/heist-one) | React + **Phaser**, TypeScript, MIT, 9★, 2026 | The **closest match to your premise**: a museum with 6 guards, **3 access-controlled doors, disguises, credentials** ("someone with the right jacket carries the wrong badge"), a graded guard **suspicion score**, light control and noise decoys. Good model for the badge-gate and "wrong ID" logic. |
| [TheFadGhost/night-ward](https://github.com/TheFadGhost/night-ward) | JS (three.js top-down), MIT, 2026 | The best **suspicion meter** reference. Cones are colour-coded (grey patrol, amber suspicion, red chase), with a visible suspicion arc and graded exposure by distance and cone edge. Guards go glance → investigate → alert. Surfaces have loudness, and an incident log says *why* you were spotted. The README says it was written largely by an AI model. [Play](https://thefadghost.github.io/night-ward/) |
| [jehualli/frontera](https://github.com/jehualli/frontera) | **Phaser 3.90** + TypeScript, no licence file, 2026 | A clean architecture to copy: `vision.ts` (line of sight, ray marching, cone tests), `Patrol.ts` (waypoint → chase → search state machine), and a `config.ts` holding every tuning number. Pixel art is generated procedurally. The README recommends Tiled JSON for hand-authored maps. **No licence, so read only. Do not copy code.** |
| [williamchen-pm/operation-good-boi](https://github.com/williamchen-pm/operation-good-boi) | **Phaser 3** + Vite, MIT code (art excluded), 2026 | **Vision cones with prop occlusion**, patrol spreading, **feet-based collision and Y-sort depth** (useful for a 3/4 top-down look), and dynamic lighting. [Play](https://operationgoodboi.com). |
| [eoinmcg/kleptokitty](https://github.com/eoinmcg/kleptokitty) | LittleJS, MIT, js13k 2025 | A tiny, readable top-down heist (keys, loot, escape, guards). Comes with a **map editor** and a [postmortem](https://eoinmcgrath.com/kleptokitty/postmortem). Mobile-friendly. |
| [kiedtl/roguelike](https://github.com/kiedtl/roguelike) (Oathbreaker) | Zig, GPL-3.0, 144★ | A mature **prison escape**: "you are a prisoner … must escape before your execution". Enemies call for help and alert the whole floor. Good for alarm propagation. |
| [anaseto/harmonist](https://github.com/anaseto/harmonist) | Go (runs in the browser), ISC, 90★ | A mature stealth design with **cones of view, light and noise**, and terrain you can hide in. Now at codeberg. |
| [shinovon/TheEscapists-j2me](https://github.com/shinovon/TheEscapists-j2me) | Java, MIT, 33★ | A fan demake whose **"NPC behavior is finished"**: a readable reimplementation of the original Escapists routine and guard logic. It needs the original game files, so treat it only as a study of mechanics. |
| [wiserim/phaser-raycaster](https://github.com/wiserim/phaser-raycaster) | Phaser 3/4 plugin, MIT, 104★ | A ready-made **raycast vision cone / field-of-view** library, if you build with Phaser. |

**Gaps:** no open-source JS game does **crowd blending** or **item handoff** well. heist-one's disguise and credential system is the nearest. For crowd blending you would probably design it yourself, for example a suspicion multiplier that drops when the player is inside a cluster of NPC students heading to class. **[inference / design suggestion]**

### Pixel-art asset packs

- **LimeZu "Modern Interiors"** ([itch.io](https://limezu.itch.io/moderninteriors)). The free version is **not for commercial use**. The paid version (a pay-what-you-want minimum, which commenters put at about $1.20 to $1.50 over the years, and recently listed in a 3-pack bundle at $5) allows commercial use **with credit**. The creator has described it as CC-BY-style. It comes at 16×16 base size, and the paid version reportedly adds larger scales. It includes classroom, library and office themes, plus a character generator that is said to be **GPL-3**. Sources: creator replies in the [comments](https://limezu.itch.io/moderninteriors/comments?after=457). The itch.io page itself blocked automated reading (403), so **check the current price and licence on the page**.
- **LimeZu "Modern Exteriors"** ([itch.io](https://limezu.itch.io/modernexteriors), [GameDev Market](https://gamedevmarket.net/asset/modern-exteriors-rpg-tileset-16x16)). It covers city streets and buildings. The minimum price was about $2.50 (itch.io, with 2026 sales at $2.50 down from $5) or $3.00 (GDM). The licence asks for credit to LimeZu with a link. Devlog v41.9 says that update is the last theme. Sources: [comments](https://limezu.itch.io/modernexteriors/comments?after=199), [devlog](https://limezu.itch.io/modernexteriors/devlog). Same caveat: check the live page.
- **LimeZu "Modern Office"** ([itch.io](https://limezu.itch.io/modernoffice)) is a companion pack, useful for the reception desk and admin areas. I did not check its price.
- **Kenney "RPG Urban Pack"** ([kenney.nl](https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-urban-pack)): **CC0, free, 16×16**, with 480+ tiles: roads, vehicles, buildings and 6 four-direction walking characters. It works as a free fallback or prototype set.
- **Kenney "Roguelike Modern City"** ([OpenGameArt](https://opengameart.org/content/roguelike-modern-city-pack)): **CC0**, 1,036 sprites. The tile size was not verified.

**Note:** neither LimeZu pack has a green faceted glass tube. **[inference]** You will need to draw the Plug-Over and the green cladding yourself, which is also what will make the level readable as IFM.
